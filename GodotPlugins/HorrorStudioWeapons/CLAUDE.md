# HorrorStudio Weapons: notes for Claude

The studio's own Godot plugin for weapons held in first person (DECISIONS 2026-11-09, the user's `weapon-system`: "create
a shot gun asset and have its pov in godots horror engine similar to the screenshot, it should be equi[pp]able, can
shoot, and reloads like a shotgun with shells entering it"; "I don't need a hand holding the gun"; then a double barrel
from their own model; then, last, "I don't want the single barrel shotgun, only the double barrel one"). It lives here:
`tools/godot_byhand.py` copies `addons/horrorstudio_weapons/` into every project made by hand and enables it. The desk's
export and the Director's projects don't copy it yet (nothing in the runtime uses it).

## Rules

- **No hands** (the user's). The reload's shells move by themselves: `_pop_in` from behind the breech and a little over
  it, along the bore into the chamber.
- **Never three flashes in a second** (the flash limit): two barrels as fast as they're pulled (0.24 s apart), then the
  reload (1.6 s). WEAPONS fires as fast as it can and counts.
- **Gun-frame numbers come from the sockets**, never from the mesh: the prop generator's sockets (`socket_<name>`) are
  the truth, so a remade gun (another barrel length) holds itself.
- **The flash is the user's way** (DECISIONS 2026-11-09, latest): wings of slightly elongated intersecting spheres,
  fronts culled, the noise triplanar on each, burnt by the facing through a colour burn with the noise as the base:
  burn(noise, facing) = 1 - (1 - noise) / facing (the other way round makes a flat white core with holes: LESSONS).
- **Smoke never takes a light at full strength from a few centimetres** (`smoke_light.gdshaderinc`'s cap, the look's
  `smoke_light_cap`), **and a cloud converges to its lit colour** (the puffs premultiplied, `occlude`): lit by the flash
  beside it, smoke glowed as a ball the size of the room, and a second barrel's flash through the first's smoke was a
  white ball. An impact's dust only adds (`occlude` 0): it never hides the wall.
- **Smoke is one noise in the world, eroded** (docs/research/muzzle_smoke.md, LESSONS): every sprite and puff on a spot
  reads the same value of `smoke_noise.gdshaderinc` and is worn away by it, its own body only its soft footprint; a
  sprite's own seed or its own erosion made beads, rope or grit. The trail is sprites along a smoothed curve, never a
  strip (a strip folds in a curl).
- **The pattern spreads from the muzzle**, on the view's line (LESSONS): its sizes are a real gun's at the muzzle's
  distance; the wall damage follows docs/research/shotgun_damage.md (by distance: one torn hole and the powder, a torn
  middle and the wad, separate holes; lead smears on hard surfaces).
- **A shell's kind travels with it**: each barrel and the pocket keep kinds; `loaded` and `spare` keep their meaning
  (scripts and checks set them: the chambers follow `loaded` with the normal kind).
- **A thing breaks into its own mesh, cut when it loads** (HSBlowUp): never a generic animation, never cut when it's
  shot (a thread at load: a shot before then waits for it). Its pieces keep its materials; the broken faces are the
  inside's. A hollow kind's closed shell is a thin wall, never filled. Pieces under TINY (4.5 cm) fall by rays; rigid
  ones' colliders are at least 6 cm along every axis and they're laid down onto the floor at rest (LESSONS).
- **A harmless kind hurts nothing** (DECISIONS 2026-11-11: "this wouldnt damage the npc but have some kind of cute
  effect"): `damage` 0 is the whole switch. HSHitReact, HSShootable and HSBlowUp each take such hits out before they
  count anything (`touched` instead); a new thing that listens to `fired` must do the same. The easter eggs' marks are
  whole (a print, a wet patch): never a crater, a tint or a smear, never the wall's dust or chips, and they fade.
- **An emitter is placed before it starts** (LESSONS, 2026-11-11): a CPUParticles3D emitting as it enters the tree
  throws its first particles from where it is then (the scene's origin). `_burst` makes it with `emitting` off, places
  it, starts it (`go` false: the caller starts it, after setting more of it); HSShootable's sparks and HSHitReact's
  hearts and drips the same. Until this, every impact's debris came out at (0, 0, 0).
- **Nobody bleeds red** (the user's): HSHitReact's ichor is black and glossy with embers, its smoke black; a new look is
  an HSWoundLook, never blood.
- **Her hitboxes are sized by her own bones** (DECISIONS 2026-11-11; HITBOXES, `python tools/godot/hitbox_check.py`):
  `_measure()` takes her legs' length, her arms', her shoulders' width and her head's own length (its bone to its
  `_null` end, else eye to eye) against MHR's adult (`ADULT`), each snapped to 1 within an eighth (an adult of any rig
  keeps the capsules it had: WEAPONS' numbers don't move), and `_make_hitboxes` scales each capsule's radius and the few
  fixed lengths by its part's (`_size`); `girth` times the torso's, neck's and limbs' radii for flesh. A child's head is
  an adult's on a smaller body: it never shrinks with her height. Don't size anything by her height alone.
- **Her hitboxes are on layer 11** (`HSHitReact.HITBOX_LAYER`): the gun's rays and a blast's query use `HSGun.SHOT_MASK`
  (layers 1 and 11); everything else (the player, her movement, sight, the hulls) stays on layer 1. **Only while she's
  seen** (`_sync_boxes` in `_physics_process`: hidden, their layers 0; down, `_gone` and `restore` keep them): tutorial
  v2's hunter waits hidden at 308's door and stopped pellets there (DECISIONS 2026-11-10, after that).
- **The ichor is alive where it can be**: HSOoze on Forward+, the plain `fx/ichor_*` decals elsewhere (the look's
  `ooze` off, the compatibility renderer). Its field is looked at once a pixel and each kind is a shader of its own (three
  looks a pixel in one shader of all four took the CPU renderer minutes to compile: LESSONS); its surface's normal
  comes from the depth buffer (the normal-roughness buffer is filled only for some materials: LESSONS). HSHitReact never
  chars, crumbles or tilts an HSOoze of hers as if it were her body.
- **Smoke that rides a body starts after its shove** (`SMOKE_LATE`): it trails after her as she walks, but thrown back
  0.4 m in a fifth of a second it drew a line through the air (LESSONS).
- **She never fades out**: charred all over, her surfaces are swapped for the opaque, cut-out crumble (a transparent mesh
  and its blended overlay drew in no fixed order: her white skin over the char, LESSONS).
- **Small debris never as a RigidBody3D on a level's floor**: under 2 cm thick it sinks through a trimesh floor in the
  physics engine's penetration slop (LESSONS); the hulls fall and roll by rays, three of them (a point slips through
  the gap a door's collider leaves at its jamb).
