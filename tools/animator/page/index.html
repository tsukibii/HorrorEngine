Animator v2

  
Export a character from the Character Studio (Export for Godot), pick it here, move the sliders, then Bake. The clips are made from the character's own body: its leg length sets the step, its weight the pace and how deep it sinks into each step, its width how far the arms are carried from the body. Nothing is recorded motion.

  
The baked file (Exports/characters/<name>/<name>.glb) carries idle, walk, run, sitting, lying down to sleep, opening a door and reaching, each a named animation. In Godot the starter kit's motion.gd plays them: walk to a marker, sit, sleep, open, pick up, by a quest's own words.

  
Drag to turn the view, the wheel to come closer, right-drag to move it. Space plays and pauses.
