KIMODO SHOT LIST

How to work through it
  1. Start the demo from the project folder: python tools/kimodo.py demo, then open http://127.0.0.1:7860.
  2. For each motion below: paste its prompt, set its length (seconds; frames 0 to N-1 at 30 fps), and add the constraints it lists: the root path (metres: ahead, left of where the body starts, facing up in the sketch), the full-body keyframes for poses Kimodo doesn't know, Loop for idles and walk cycles. Your blocking overlay frames it in the room.
  3. Generate; change the seed or raise the samples for other takes. Turn post-processing on when the feet or the constraints matter.
  4. Load/Save → Motion → paste the save path (copy button) → Save Motion as NPZ.
  5. Press Import saved motions (or python tools/motion.py --game G import): the desk plays them chained on their clips.
Never prompt where to look: the look layer turns the head toward the player (or a tracker) on top. One behaviour per motion, 2 to 10 s (docs/KIMODO.md).
