Accessories

Made here, no models (tools/character/accessories.js): glasses from the eyes and the face's own points; a piercing, a sticker or the eyepatch held to the skin where it sits, so it follows every expression (put it on a spot from the list, or Place by clicking on the body); a choker round the neck as it is. Saved with the character as plain settings: change any of it later.

Clothes

Cut from this body (tools/character/tailor.js, after Woohill's character creator): the skin under a garment lifted by its ease and relaxed into cloth, skirts hung from the waist as rings, the details lifted off it. Each garment goes over the ones above it in the list. They refit when the body changes. Designs: Content/Studio/Clothes/designs.json.
