# The sampled instruments' sources

Every sample here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain): free to use, change and ship in the game, commercially, with no
credit required. `music/tools/fetch_samples.py` downloads and prepares them (trimmed, faded, one gain per instrument,
mono Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz); `instruments.json` is the sampler's map.

| Instruments | Source |
| --- | --- |
| piano, guitar, steel | Meta Open Sound (github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound), CC0 1.0 |
| harp, glock, marimba, xylo, bells, pizz, strings, trem, flute, flute_stac, horn, timpani, drums | VSCO-2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios (github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), CC0 1.0 |

Full-quality WAVs: Meta Open Sound's releases (24-bit / 48 kHz, with SFZ maps) and the VSCO-2 CE repository.
